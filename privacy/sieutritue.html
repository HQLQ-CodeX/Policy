Privacy Policy

Game: Brainy: Mind Puzzle Quest | Publisher: HQL Global | Effective Date: October 2026

Welcome to Brainy: Mind Puzzle Quest ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us"), owned and published by HQL Global. We are committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of all our players. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application on iOS and Android devices.

1. Information We Collect

Brainy: Mind Puzzle Quest is designed primarily as an offline, single-player casual puzzle game. We do not require you to register an account, log in with social media credentials, or provide your real name, physical address, or phone number.

However, like most mobile applications, we integrate standard industry SDKs to provide analytics, performance monitoring, and advertisements:

  • Gameplay & Progress Data: Game scores, level completions, star ratings, and sound/music preferences are saved locally on your device via secure local storage.
  • Device & Usage Information: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic data including device model, operating system version, locale/language settings, screen resolution, and crash logs.
  • Analytics Identifiers: Anonymous installation tokens and gameplay event telemetry (e.g., stage completed, stage failed) used exclusively to balance puzzle difficulty and fix technical bugs.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Standard mobile advertising IDs (such as Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA/IDFV) if permitted by your device consent settings, used to serve contextual or personalized advertisements.

2. Third-Party Services & SDKs

To help deliver a seamless experience, we partner with trusted third-party service providers. These services operate under their own independent privacy policies:

  • Google Mobile Ads & UMP (AdMob): Used to display in-game ads (banners, interstitials, and rewarded videos). Google AdMob utilizes the User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain and manage user consent in regions subject to GDPR, UK GDPR, and California privacy regulations.

  • PostHog Analytics: Used for telemetry and aggregate gameplay event tracking to improve user experience and monitor game stability. PostHog tracks anonymized in-game actions without collecting personally identifiable information (PII).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To enable in-game mechanics, save progression, and maintain game settings.
  • To analyze gameplay trends, optimize puzzle difficulty, and fix technical crashes or glitches.
  • To display non-intrusive advertisements that support the free continuous development of the game.
  • To comply with legal obligations and store platform guidelines (Google Play Policies & Apple App Store Review Guidelines).

4. Advertising & User Consent (GDPR / CCPA)

In jurisdictions where consent is legally mandated (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and California):

  • We display a Consent Form powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) on the initial app launch.
  • You may choose between personalized ads, non-personalized ads, or manage individual partner permissions.
  • You can revoke or modify your consent at any time inside the app via the Privacy Settings / Ad Choices button.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Brainy: Mind Puzzle Quest is designed for a general audience of all ages (Family-Friendly / Casual). We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us without appropriate consent, please contact us immediately at support@hqlglobal.com, and we will promptly delete such information.

6. Data Retention & User Data Deletion

Your Right to Data Deletion: Because all game progress is stored locally on your device, you can delete your gameplay data at any time simply by clearing the application storage in your device settings or uninstalling the Game.

If you wish to request the deletion of any anonymous analytics or diagnostic data that may be associated with your device identifier, please send an email with your request to support@hqlglobal.com. We will process your request within 30 days in accordance with applicable laws.

7. Security

We implement reasonable administrative and technical security measures to protect the integrity of user data. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
