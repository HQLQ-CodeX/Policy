1. Overview & Scope

Welcome to Brainy: Mind Puzzle Quest (also known locally as Brainy: Thử Thách Trí Tuệ, application package com.hqlglobal.sieutritue, "the Game", "we", "our", or "us"), owned and published by HQL Global.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through transparent policies. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, and protection when you download, install, and play our mobile puzzle game on Android (Google Play) and iOS (Apple App Store) devices.

2. Information We Collect

Brainy: Mind Puzzle Quest is designed primarily as a casual, offline-friendly single-player puzzle game. We do not require user registration, account creation, social logins, nor do we request or collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your legal name, home address, or phone number.

When you play the Game, basic diagnostic, gameplay, and technical data may be processed locally or through verified third-party SDKs:

  • Gameplay & Progress Data: Scores, levels unlocked, star ratings, and sound/music toggles are saved locally on your device via secure sandbox storage. This data is not uploaded to our external servers.
  • Device & Diagnostic Information: Non-personally identifiable technical telemetry such as device model, OS version, language setting, screen resolution, and crash logs to ensure high stability across various device configurations.
  • Analytics Identifiers: A locally generated pseudonymous installation token used exclusively by our analytics framework to track gameplay completion rates and balance puzzle difficulty.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Standard advertising IDs (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID on Android or IDFA on iOS) if permitted by your device consent settings, used strictly for fraud prevention, frequency capping, and ad delivery.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the processed data solely for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To enable in-game mechanics, save progression, and maintain player settings.
  • To analyze gameplay trends, optimize puzzle difficulty, and fix technical crashes or glitches.
  • To display advertisements that support free access and continuous development of the game.
  • To comply with legal obligations and platform policies (Google Play Developer Policies & Apple App Store Review Guidelines).

5. Advertising & User Consent (GDPR / CCPA)

In regions where consent is legally required (including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and California):

  • We display a Consent Management Dialog powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) upon initial launch.
  • You may choose between personalized ads, non-personalized ads, or manage partner preferences individually.
  • You can revoke or adjust your consent choices at any time inside the game settings via the Privacy / Ad Choices button.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Brainy: Mind Puzzle Quest is designed for a general, family-friendly audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

If a parent or guardian believes their child has provided personal information without appropriate authorization, please contact us at support@hqlglobal.com, and we will promptly delete the data.

7. Data Retention & User Data Deletion Rights

Your Right to Data Deletion: Because all game save data is stored locally in your device's sandbox, you can completely erase all gameplay records at any time simply by clearing the application storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the application.

To request the deletion of any anonymous analytics or diagnostic telemetry that may be associated with your temporary installation identifier, please contact our support team at support@hqlglobal.com. We will process your deletion request within 30 days in compliance with applicable data privacy laws.

8. Security

We implement appropriate administrative and technical safeguards to protect the integrity of processed data. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% impenetrable.
