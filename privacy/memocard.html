Privacy Policy for MemoMatch

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game MemoMatch (including "MemoMatch: Memory Card Game" and "MemoMatch: Lật Hình Tìm Cặp"), developed and operated by LCSoft ("we", "us", or "our").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

MemoMatch does not require user registration or account creation. We do not collect or store personal identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

To provide and keep this game free to play, we display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks. These third-party services may collect certain device and usage information, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID) or Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA)
  • Device information (hardware model, operating system version)
  • Approximate IP address and general geolocation data
  • In-app interaction data and ad performance statistics

We utilize the following third-party service provider:

  • Google AdMob & Google Mobile Ads SDK: Used for serving banner and interstitial ads. Please review Google's privacy policy here: https://policies.google.com/privacy and Google Ads Privacy.

3. Consent Management (GDPR & ATT)

In accordance with European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT), our app integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK have the right to grant or revoke consent for personalized ads at any time via the in-app "Privacy Options" menu on the home screen.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

MemoMatch is designed as a casual brain-training puzzle game for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately so we can remove such information.

5. Local Data Storage

The app saves game preferences (such as selected language and sound settings) locally on your device using on-device storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no electronic transmission over the internet or storage method is 100% secure.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
