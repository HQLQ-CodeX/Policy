Summary

This app is a child-directed educational map quiz app. The app does not require an account and SUNSKY does not knowingly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, photos, contacts, or messages from children or other users.

Information Processed

The app may process basic app usage data on the device so that quizzes, learning screens, sounds, and language choices work correctly. This data is not sent to a SUNSKY server.

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google Mobile Ads SDK may process device identifiers, advertising ID where available, IP address, approximate location, app information, ad interactions, diagnostics, and similar data needed to request, deliver, limit, and measure ads.

Children and Ads

The app requests child-directed treatment for ad requests and sets the maximum ad content rating to G. Ads are intended to be appropriate for children and families.

Sharing

SUNSKY does not sell personal information and does not share user-provided personal data. Data handled by Google AdMob is processed by Google under Google's policies and controls.

Retention and Deletion

SUNSKY does not keep server-side user profiles for this app. Parents or users may reset the Android advertising ID or limit ad personalization in device settings. Privacy questions or deletion requests can be sent to the contact email above.

Security

The app does not ask children to submit personal information. We keep the app's third-party SDKs updated where practical and limit data processing to features needed for the app and ads.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app, ads, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are made.
