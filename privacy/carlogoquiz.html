Policy Availability

This page is a public, non-editable HTML privacy policy for the app identified above. The Google Play listing and the app should link directly to this app-specific page, not to a PDF, document editor, login page, or a page containing multiple unrelated policies.

Scope

This policy applies only to the specific app identified above. If SUNSKY publishes other apps, each app has its own privacy policy page so users and reviewers can verify the correct app name, package name, developer name, and contact information.

Summary

This app is a child-directed educational logo quiz app. The app does not require an account and SUNSKY does not knowingly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, photos, contacts, or messages from children or other users.

Information Processed

The app may process basic app usage data on the device so that quiz screens, learning screens, sounds, and language choices work correctly. This data is not sent to a SUNSKY server.

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google Mobile Ads SDK may process device identifiers, advertising ID where available, IP address, approximate location, app information, ad interactions, diagnostics, and similar data needed to request, deliver, limit, and measure ads.

Children and Ads

The app requests child-directed treatment for ad requests and sets the maximum ad content rating to G. The app is designed for children and families. SUNSKY does not create behavioral advertising profiles, does not ask children to provide personal information, and does not use precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, health, financial, or account data for ads.

Permissions

The app may use Internet and network state permissions so educational content and ads can load correctly. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may add the Android advertising ID permission on supported devices. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, call log, calendar, precise location, or external account permissions.

Third-party Services

The app uses Google AdMob and Google Play services. These services are provided by Google and may process data under Google's privacy terms, technologies policies, and SDK disclosures. Useful references include Google Privacy Policy, Google partner sites and apps policy, and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Advertising Practices

Ad requests are configured as child-directed and G-rated. Ads may still use contextual signals such as app information, device type, coarse network/location signals, and frequency capping. SUNSKY does not knowingly allow ads that require children to provide personal information before viewing content.

Local Storage

The app may store simple local settings or learning state on the device, such as language selection, selected category, or app progress. This local data stays on the device and can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

No Accounts, Purchases, or User-generated Content

The app does not require users to create an account, does not provide in-app social features, does not let children publicly post content, and does not collect payment card or financial information. If the app opens Google Play for rating or sharing, that external screen is handled by Google Play or the selected sharing app.

Parental Rights and Requests

Parents or guardians can contact SUNSKY at the email address above to ask privacy questions, request clarification, or request deletion of any information they believe was provided by a child. Because SUNSKY does not maintain user accounts or server-side child profiles for this app, most data removal is done by clearing app data, uninstalling the app, or resetting the Android advertising ID in device settings.

Regional Privacy Notes

For users in regions with privacy laws such as the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, United States, Brazil, or other jurisdictions, SUNSKY limits its own data handling to what is necessary to provide the app. Google may provide additional privacy controls for ads and measurement through Android, Google Play services, AdMob, or the user's Google account settings.

International Processing

Google AdMob and Google Play services may process data on servers located outside the user's country or region. SUNSKY does not operate a separate user-data server for this app.

Sharing

SUNSKY does not sell personal information and does not share user-provided personal data. Data handled by Google AdMob is processed by Google under Google's policies and controls.

Retention and Deletion

SUNSKY does not keep server-side user profiles for this app. Parents or users may reset the Android advertising ID or limit ad personalization in device settings. Privacy questions or deletion requests can be sent to the contact email above.

Security

The app does not ask children to submit personal information. We keep the app's third-party SDKs updated where practical and limit data processing to features needed for the app and ads.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app, ads, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are made.
