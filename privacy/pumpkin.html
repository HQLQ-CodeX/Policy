This Privacy Policy explains how Pumpkin Survivor ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you install, play, and interact with our mobile game on Android (Google Play) and iOS (Apple App Store).

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

We respect your privacy. Pumpkin Survivor does not require user account registration, login credentials, or personally identifiable information (such as your legal name, physical address, email address, or phone number) to play the game.

Most core game features can be enjoyed offline. When your device is connected to the Internet, the game interacts with reputable third-party SDKs to provide gameplay analytics, advertisements, and optional in-app purchases.

2. Information We Collect and How We Collect It

We collect information in the following categories:

A. Gameplay & Progress Data (Stored Locally)

  • Game State & Progression: Gold, Candy balance, unlocked characters, skill evolutions, talent upgrades, high scores, and audio/language settings are stored locally on your device storage.
  • We do not sync or store your local save files on external custom servers.

B. Technical and Device Information (Anonymous Analytics)

To improve game balance, fix performance bottlenecks, and resolve crashes, we use PostHog Analytics. The data collected includes:

  • Randomized Device Identifier: A unique pseudo-anonymous GUID generated locally upon first installation (`distinct_id`). This identifier is not linked to your real identity.
  • Device & OS Specifications: Device model, operating system version, game version, and system language.
  • In-Game Telemetry Events: Stage start/completion, survival duration, defeat causes, boss completions, level-up choices, and in-game economy actions (gold/candy earned or spent).
  • IP Address Handling: Client IP data is automatically discarded and not retained in analytics dashboards.

Opt-Out Option: You can disable analytics data collection anytime via: Settings → Share Analytics (Turn OFF).

C. In-App Purchases (IAP)

The game offers optional in-app purchases (such as Candy packs and No-Ads package). We use RevenueCat to facilitate in-app purchase validation:

  • Payment Processing: All transactions are processed directly by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play Store). We never access or store your credit card or financial details.
  • Purchase Validation: RevenueCat receives anonymous transaction receipts to verify purchase status, grant in-game entitlements, and support the "Restore Purchases" feature.

D. Advertisements

To support free access, the game integrates Rewarded and Interstitial ads via Google AdMob:

  • Google AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier (GAID on Android or IDFA on iOS if tracking permission is granted) to deliver relevant ads and combat ad fraud.
  • GDPR / Consent Management (EEA/UK): We display the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog on initial launch. You can update your consent preferences anytime via Settings → Privacy Options.
  • No Ads Package: Purchasing the No Ads package removes interstitial ads and lets you claim rewarded bonuses immediately without viewing video ads.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

The game incorporates official SDKs from the following reputable providers. Please review their privacy policies for further details:

Service Purpose Privacy Policy Link
Google AdMob In-game advertising network & consent management (UMP) Google Privacy Policy
RevenueCat In-app purchase validation & receipt verification RevenueCat Privacy Policy
PostHog Anonymous game telemetry and performance monitoring PostHog Privacy Policy

4. Data Retention and Security

  • All network communications between our third-party SDK partners and their backend servers use secure HTTPS/TLS encryption. Local game data is stored in your device's protected app sandbox.
  • Local gameplay data remains on your device until you clear the application data or uninstall the application.
  • Telemetry data collected by analytics is stored on secured servers and retained only as long as necessary for game optimization.

5. Children's Privacy

Pumpkin Survivor is designed for general audiences (rated PEGI 7 / Everyone 10+ / iOS 9+). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to remove such information.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as GDPR in Europe or CCPA/CPRA in California), you have rights regarding your data:

  • Turn Off Analytics: In the game's Settings menu, toggle off "Share Analytics".
  • Revoke Ad Consent: In the game's Settings menu, click "Privacy Options" to reopen the consent form and adjust your choices.
  • Delete Game Data: Uninstalling the application removes all local game saves and resets local identifiers.
  • Right to Inquire / Data Deletion: You can contact us at our support email below to request information or request deletion of data associated with your anonymous ID.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Effective Date" at the top. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

A. Dữ liệu chơi game & Lưu trữ tiến trình (Lưu cục bộ)

  • Tiến trình chơi: Số vàng, kẹo, nhân vật đã mở khóa, nâng cấp kỹ năng, cây thiên phú và cài đặt ngôn ngữ/âm thanh được lưu trữ trực tiếp trên bộ nhớ thiết bị của bạn.

B. Thông tin kỹ thuật & Phân tích gameplay (PostHog Analytics)

Để theo dõi độ cân bằng game, sửa lỗi crash và cải thiện trải nghiệm, chúng tôi sử dụng công cụ phân tích PostHog:

  • Mã định danh ngẫu nhiên (GUID): Một chuỗi ngẫu nhiên (`distinct_id`) được tạo tự động khi bạn cài game lần đầu. Chuỗi này hoàn toàn ẩn danh và không liên kết với danh tính cá nhân.
  • Thông số thiết bị: Dòng máy, phiên bản hệ điều hành, ngôn ngữ máy và phiên bản game.
  • Sự kiện trong game: Bắt đầu màn chơi, thời lượng sống sót, nguyên nhân thua, chọn thẻ nâng cấp, tiêu dùng kẹo/vàng trong game.
  • Bảo mật địa chỉ IP: Cấu hình hệ thống tự động loại bỏ và không lưu trữ địa chỉ IP của người dùng.

Quyền tắt phân tích: Bạn có thể chủ động tắt chia sẻ dữ liệu phân tích bất cứ lúc nào trong: Cài đặt (Settings) → Chia sẻ phân tích (Share Analytics: TẮT).

  • Xác thực gói mua: RevenueCat nhận biên lai kỹ thuật số và mã giao dịch từ kho ứng dụng để cấp quyền vật phẩm trong game và hỗ trợ khôi phục giao dịch (Restore Purchases).

D. Quảng cáo trong game (Google AdMob)

Để duy trì trải nghiệm miễn phí, game tích hợp quảng cáo có thưởng (Rewarded Ads) và quảng cáo xen kẽ (Interstitial Ads) qua Google AdMob:

  • Người mua gói No Ads: Sẽ không hiển thị quảng cáo xen kẽ và nhận ngay quà tặng có thưởng mà không phải xem quảng cáo.
