Google Play Setup Checklist

  • Use the exact app-specific privacy policy URL in the Google Play store listing.
  • Link the same privacy policy URL inside the app.
  • Keep the developer name in Google Play consistent with the developer name shown here: SUNSKY.
  • Keep Google Play Data safety answers aligned with each policy, especially AdMob device IDs, app activity, diagnostics, approximate location, and ad interactions.
  • Keep ads configured as child-directed and maximum ad content rating G for child-directed apps.
